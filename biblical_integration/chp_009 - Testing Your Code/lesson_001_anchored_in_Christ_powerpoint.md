# Anchored in Christ

## The Assertive Spirit of God

In a limited sense, the Holy Spirit behaves a little bit like an assert statement regarding God's gospel call to the world. When the Word is preached, the world's view of itself and of its standing before God is challenged. Sinners are reminded that they do not stand justly before a Holy God. This is part of the work of the Holy Spirit in the preaching of the Word. The conviction of sin, however, is not merely diagnostic but is part of the effectual call of God. A great contrast that we find between the work of the Spirit and the Python assert statement is that the Spirit is personal and active. He is not simply a feature of language or a force to be controlled and manipulated by the tongues of men and women.​

### Primary Verse

"And when He has come, He will convict the world of sin, and of righteousness, and of judgment."​

John 16:8, NKJV​
